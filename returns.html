Returns & remakes

Every LUMIA shade is cut and sewn for one window, to the measurements you enter. That is why our policy is built around remakes, not stock returns.

If we got it wrong, we remake it — free

If what arrives is not what you ordered, the fix costs you nothing. That covers:

  • a manufacturing defect — a fault in fabric, stitching, mechanism or motor;
  • a wrong build — the size, fabric, colour or options differ from your order confirmation;
  • damage in transit — the shade arrived damaged in the box.

Tell us within 30 days of delivery (within 5 days for transit damage), with a photo of the issue and of the shipping label. We remake the piece and ship it to you free of charge. You usually keep or recycle the original — no need to post anything back unless we ask.

What a remake does not cover

  • Mis-measurement on your side. We make exactly the size that is ordered, so please measure twice — our size guides are on each product page. If a shade fits badly because the entered measurements were off, the remake is not free; still, write to us: depending on the piece we can often re-cut or remake it at a reduced price.
  • Change of mind about fabric or colour after production has started. Order free swatches or ask us for fabric photos before you buy if you are unsure.
  • Normal wear, misuse, or damage from installation.

Cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund any time before production starts. We review every order before cutting — usually within one business day — so if you spot a mistake, message us right away from your account and we will hold the order.

How to start a remake

Open your account, select the order and use “Ask a question about this order”, or write to lumiashades@gmail.com with your order reference (it looks like HOME-123456-7890). We answer within one business day.

The short version. Custom-made goods cannot go back on a shelf, so we do not offer change-of-mind returns — but if the error is ours, in any way, you get a new one free. That is the deal, in plain words.
