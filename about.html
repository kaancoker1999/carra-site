About us

LUMIA has been building made-to-measure window treatments since 1999. Not assembling them — building them, mechanism included.

Cutting, sewing, cell bonding, motor and mechanism assembly and final cycle testing all happen in one plant. Orders leave the line as finished, boxed units, addressed to the window they were measured for.

We supply large online retailers, independent dealers and distributors across North America and Europe, under our own brands and as private label.

Standards we build to

— Compliance & child safety
EN 13120ANSI/WCMA A100.1OEKO-TEX 100REACHISO 9001

Child safety. Every LUMIA shade is built to the EN 13120 and ANSI/WCMA A100.1 child-safety standards. Our cordless drives — including cordless top-down bottom-up — have no accessible cord anywhere on the unit, and continuous cord loops ship with a wall tension anchor that must be installed. For homes with young children we recommend cordless or motorized operation on every window.
