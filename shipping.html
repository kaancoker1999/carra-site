Shipping & delivery

Every LUMIA shade is made for one specific window — yours. Here is how it gets there.

01 · Made

10–15 business days

Your order is cut, sewn, assembled and tested in our own workshop after a final review of the measurements.

02 · Shipped

Free within the US

Standard shipping is free on every order, to all 50 states. No minimums, no surcharges at checkout.

03 · Tracked

Door to door

The moment your order ships, the tracking number appears in your account and you can follow it to your door.

Production time

Because every piece is made to the exact size you enter, production starts after we review your order — usually within one business day — and takes 10–15 business days. Motorized shades are cycle-tested before they are boxed.

Shipping

  • Cost: free standard shipping on every order within the United States.
  • Coverage: we currently ship to US addresses only. Your delivery address is entered on the secure payment page.
  • Carrier & tracking: orders ship with a tracked carrier service; the tracking number is shown in your account and works on the carrier's site.

When it arrives

Open the box within a few days of delivery and check each shade. If anything arrived damaged in transit, message us from your account or write to lumiashades@gmail.com within 5 days with a photo — we will remake the damaged piece free of charge. Details are in our returns & remakes policy.

A note on timing. “10–15 business days” is production, not a promise date for delivery; transit adds a few days depending on where you are. If a fabric is temporarily out of stock we tell you within two business days of your order, with the choice of waiting or switching fabrics.
