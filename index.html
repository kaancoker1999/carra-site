Motion, built in

— Motorization

A motorized shade whose quality can be seen and (barely) heard.

Every LUMIA motorized unit is driven by Motionblinds — the smart motor technology by Coulisse — matched to our own mechanisms and assembled in our plant. Rechargeable lithium or hardwired, built on Matter over Thread, with app, remote and hub control — and both rails addressable independently on top-down bottom-up units. For Apple Home over Thread, we also build with Eve MotionBlinds drives.

Every motorized unit is cycle-tested before it is boxed, and every drive is serviceable in the field without taking the shade off the wall.

Two rails, one window

— Top-down bottom-up

Daylight at the top, privacy at the bottom, on the same shade.

A top-down bottom-up shade — TDBU — has two independently moving rails, so it opens from the top, the bottom, or both at once. Daylight comes in over the top of the fabric while the lower half of the window stays covered. It is the single feature homeowners ask for by name once they have seen it work.

Available made to measure on our Roman shades and cellular shades — cordless, cord loop or motorized.

— An industry first

Top-down bottom-up, on a Roman shade.

TDBU has been available on cellular shades for years. On Roman shades it has effectively not existed, because the fabric is heavier, the fold has to stay square, and the second rail has nowhere to hide. LUMIA was the first manufacturer to bring it to market on Roman shades — and we did it on all three drive types rather than one.

TD · C

Cordless

Spring-balanced on both rails. No accessible cord anywhere on the unit.

TD · L

Cord loop

Tensioned continuous loop with wall anchor. For heavier fabrics and tall drops.

TD · M

Motorized

Both rails driven and addressable independently, by app, remote or schedule.
