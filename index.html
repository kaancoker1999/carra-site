About us

— The company

CARRA has been building made-to-measure window treatments since 1999. Not assembling them — building them, mechanism included.

Cutting, sewing, cell bonding, motor and mechanism assembly and final cycle testing all happen in one plant. Orders leave the line as finished, boxed units, addressed to the window they were measured for.

We supply large online retailers, independent dealers and distributors across North America and Europe, under our own brands and as private label. What the buyer gets from us is not a catalogue — it is a production slot with a lead time they can publish on their own site.

— Motorization

A motorised shade is only as good as the quietest moment in its travel.

Every CARRA motorised unit is driven by Motionblinds — the smart motor technology by Coulisse — matched to our own mechanisms and assembled in our plant. Rechargeable lithium or hardwired, built on Matter over Thread, with app, remote and hub control — and both rails addressable independently on top-down bottom-up units. For Apple Home over Thread, we also build with Eve MotionBlinds drives.

Every motorised unit is cycle-tested before it is boxed, and every drive is serviceable in the field without taking the shade off the wall.

Two rails, one window

— Top-down bottom-up

Daylight at the top, privacy at the bottom, on the same shade.

Top-down bottom-up lets a room keep both at once — the light comes over the top of the fabric while the lower half of the window stays covered. It is the single feature homeowners ask for by name once they have seen it work.

It is also the hardest thing to build well, because a second moving rail has to travel true, hold position at any height, and do it without an accessible cord.

— An industry first

Top-down bottom-up, on a Roman shade.

TDBU has been available on cellular shades for years. On Roman shades it has effectively not existed, because the fabric is heavier, the fold has to stay square, and the second rail has nowhere to hide. CARRA was the first manufacturer to bring it to market on Roman shades — and we did it on all three drive types rather than one.
